# Dhriti: 2-minute demo script
Before recording: server running, `Invoke-RestMethod http://127.0.0.1:5000/api/health` shows ai True, send one test chat, clear site data, use fictional details only.
0:00 Home. Tap "Start getting help".
0:10 Choose "Blackmail or threats". Safety check: danger No, know them Yes. Show "Show my plan".
0:25 Plan: read steps, "Provisions that may apply" (disclaimer), Verified help (112, 1930). Open the cybercrime portal link.
0:50 Tap "Record this incident" (prefilled). Add a sentence and a screenshot. Save. Point at the SHA-256 value.
1:15 "Prepare a report draft": redaction on, tick "I have read this draft", download. Say: not a filed complaint.
1:30 Sakhi: ask the same concern, show the streamed Gemini reply. Stop the server, ask again: show the labelled offline guidance and the "Show offline guidance now" button.
1:50 Help page: "How Dhriti handles your information" and the SOS note (opens the dialer, nothing automatic). Close.
Do not claim: automatic alerts, encryption, map, accounts, or that hashes prove authenticity.

# Dhriti — 2:59 Demo Video Script

### 0:00–0:15 | Introduction
**On screen:** Show the Dhriti logo, then the home dashboard.

**Narration:**
“Hello everyone! We are presenting Dhriti, a women's safety and digital-rights platform designed to help people navigate difficult situations, access reliable guidance, and take informed steps toward their safety.”

### 0:15–0:32 | The Problem
**On screen:** Show the dashboard and the available safety features.

**Narration:**
“In stressful situations, finding the right information quickly can be difficult. People may not know what steps to take, where to find reliable resources, or how to organise important incident information. Dhriti brings these tools together in one accessible platform.”

### 0:32–0:48 | Home Dashboard
**On screen:** Navigate through the dashboard and feature shortcuts.

**Narration:**
“The home dashboard provides a starting point for exploring safety resources and available tools. Our goal is to make essential information easier to find through a clear interface and straightforward navigation.”

### 0:48–1:12 | Sakhi Assistant
**On screen:** Open Sakhi and demonstrate a tested question and its response.

**Narration:**
“Sakhi is Dhriti's conversational assistant. It helps users explore safety guidance and understand possible next steps. The application integrates Gemini for AI-assisted responses and includes offline guidance when the AI service is unavailable.”

### 1:12–1:32 | Incident Navigator
**On screen:** Open Incident Navigator and select a scenario. Demonstrate its step-by-step guidance.

**Narration:**
“Incident Navigator organises guidance around different situations. Instead of presenting everything at once, it guides users through practical steps so they can understand their options and decide what to do next.”

### 1:32–1:49 | Rights Guide
**On screen:** Open Rights Guide and display a topic and its resource links.

**Narration:**
“The Rights Guide helps users explore relevant rights information and find supporting resources. Wherever possible, we direct users to official sources so they can consult the information and verify the guidance.”

### 1:49–2:08 | Evidence Timeline
**On screen:** Open the evidence section. Add a demonstration note and show the timeline or export option that works in your version.

**Narration:**
“Dhriti also includes an evidence-recording feature that helps users organise incident notes chronologically. This can make personal records easier to review and export. Users should remember that keeping a record in the application does not automatically make it legally verified evidence.”

### 2:08–2:25 | Privacy Checkup
**On screen:** Open Privacy Checkup and demonstrate the checklist.

**Narration:**
“Privacy Checkup helps users review important privacy and digital-safety considerations through a checklist. It encourages users to examine their settings and habits and identify areas where they may want to improve their digital security.”

### 2:25–2:41 | Response Simulator
**On screen:** Open a scenario, select an answer, and show the feedback.

**Narration:**
“The Response Simulator offers scenario-based practice, allowing users to consider different responses and learn from the feedback provided. This makes safety awareness more interactive rather than relying only on static information.”

### 2:41–2:59 | Conclusion
**On screen:** Return to the dashboard, show the Dhriti logo, and finish with a simple closing screen.

**Narration:**
“Dhriti brings together conversational guidance, incident navigation, rights information, evidence organisation, and privacy awareness in one platform. Our aim is to make safety information more accessible and actionable. Thank you for watching our demonstration of Dhriti.”
